You can submit a ticket to HPC support team by selecting "Submit Support Ticket" button from the dropdown menu on navigation bar.

![Help menu open in the top navigation bar, with the Submit Support Ticket option shown](../assets/ondemand/submit_ticket/submit-support-ticket-new.png){ width=100% height=100%}


Fill in the form with all the necessary information specific to your issue and click on the Submit button. This will directly open a ticket in our system and our support team will get back to you as soon as possible.

![Top part of the support ticket form with fields for name, email and the type of issue](../assets/ondemand/submit_ticket/ticket_form_1.png){ width=100% height=100%}

![Bottom part of the support ticket form with the description field and the Submit button](../assets/ondemand/submit_ticket/ticket_form_2.png){ width=100% height=100%}

!!! tip "Job specific ticket"
    You can also submit job specific ticket from you current interactive sessions.

![Interactive session card with a button for submitting a ticket about that specific job](../assets/ondemand/submit_ticket/job_specific_ticket.png){ width=100% height=100% }
